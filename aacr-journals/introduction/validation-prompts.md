# Introduction - Validation Prompts

```
Act as an experienced AACR journal editor and scientific reviewer.

I will provide the Introduction section of my manuscript.

Please evaluate it against the following AACR expectations:

AACR Introduction Requirements:
- The introduction should provide a brief overview of the background and rationale for the study.
- It is not necessary to cite all of the background literature in the introduction.
- Brief reference to the most pertinent articles generally suffices to acquaint the reader with the findings of others in the field and with the problem or question that the investigation addresses.

Review Instructions:

1. Assess whether the Introduction effectively:
   - Provides sufficient background.
   - Explains the rationale for the study.
   - Clearly defines the research problem or knowledge gap.
   - Establishes the significance of the study.
   - Maintains an appropriate level of detail for an AACR journal.

2. Identify any issues, weaknesses, or areas that may not align with AACR expectations, such as:
   - Excessive background information.
   - Too many literature citations or literature summary.
   - Missing rationale or research gap.
   - Unclear study objective.
   - Overly broad or overly detailed discussion.
   - Weak logical flow.
   - Unsupported claims.
   - Redundant statements.

3. For each issue identified, provide:
   - Exact Sentence(s): Quote the exact sentence(s) from my Introduction that triggered the comment.
   - Problem: Explain what is potentially problematic.
   - Why it matters: Explain why this may weaken compliance with AACR expectations.
   - Suggested improvement: Provide a specific recommendation for improvement, without rewriting the entire section.

4. Do NOT rewrite the entire Introduction.

5. Do NOT perform copyediting, grammar correction, paraphrasing, or language polishing unless the issue affects scientific clarity, logic, or AACR compliance.

6. Prioritize major scientific and structural issues over minor stylistic concerns.

Present your feedback using the following format:

Strengths:

- ...
```
