# Discussion - Validation Prompts

```
Act as an experienced AACR journal editor and scientific reviewer.

I will provide the Discussion section of my manuscript.

Please evaluate it against the following AACR expectations:

AACR Discussion Requirements:

- The data should be interpreted concisely.
- The Discussion should not repeat material already presented in the Results section.
- Speculation is permissible, but it must be well founded and supported by the data and existing evidence.
- Discussion of the broader scientific, clinical, or translational implications of the findings is encouraged.

Review Instructions:

1. Assess whether the Discussion effectively:
   - Interprets the study findings rather than simply restating results.
   - Explains the significance of the findings.
   - Places the findings in the context of existing literature.
   - Discusses the broader implications of the study.
   - Maintains an appropriate level of scientific rigor.
   - Provides balanced conclusions supported by the presented evidence.

2. Evaluate whether:
   - Result statements are excessively repeated without interpretation.
   - Conclusions extend beyond what the data support.
   - Speculative statements are adequately justified.
   - Alternative explanations are considered where appropriate.
   - The strengths and limitations of the study are discussed where appropriate.
   - The discussion remains focused and concise.
   - The significance and implications of the findings are clearly communicated.

3. Identify only substantive issues that may affect:
   - Scientific interpretation
   - Logical reasoning
   - Scientific rigor
   - AACR compliance
   - Reader understanding

4. For each issue identified, provide:
   - Exact Sentence(s): Quote the exact sentence(s) from my Discussion section that triggered the comment.
   - Problem: Explain the issue.
   - Why it matters: Explain why it may weaken the Discussion or conflict with AACR expectations.
   - Suggested improvement: Describe what should be clarified, strengthened, shortened, qualified, or supported. Do not rewrite the entire section.

5. Do NOT rewrite the manuscript.

6. Do NOT perform grammar correction, copyediting, or language polishing unless the issue affects scientific interpretation or clarity.

7. Only report issues that are genuinely important. Do not generate minor or speculative comments. If a section adequately satisfies AACR expectations, state that no revision is needed.

8. Pay particular attention to distinguishing:
   - Interpretation versus repetition of Results.
   - Evidence-based conclusions versus unsupported speculation.
   - Meaningful implications versus overstatement of impact.

Present your feedback using the following format:

Strengths:
- ...
- ...

Issues and Suggestions:

Issue 1
- Exact Sentence(s):
  "[quoted sentence(s)]"
- Problem:
- Why it matters:
- Suggested improvement:

Issue 2
- Exact Sentence(s):
  "[quoted sentence(s)]"
- Problem:
- Why it matters:
- Suggested improvement:

Discussion:
[PASTE TEXT HERE]
```
