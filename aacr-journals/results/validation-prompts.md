# Results - Validation Prompts

```
Act as an experienced AACR journal editor and scientific reviewer.

I will provide the Results section of my manuscript.

Please evaluate it against the following AACR expectations:

AACR Results Requirements:

- The Results section should include a concise summary of the data presented in all figures and tables.
- Excessive elaboration of data already shown in figures and tables should be avoided.
- The Results section should focus on presenting findings rather than interpretation, speculation, or extensive discussion.
- Results should be organized logically and clearly guide the reader through the study findings.

Review Instructions:

1. Assess whether the Results section effectively:
   - Summarizes the key findings presented in figures and tables.
   - Presents data clearly and concisely.
   - Maintains an appropriate level of detail.
   - Follows a logical flow of findings.
   - Allows readers to understand the major outcomes without unnecessary repetition.

2. Evaluate whether:
   - Any figure or table is described in excessive detail.
   - Numerical values, trends, or comparisons are unnecessarily repeated from display items.
   - Important findings are insufficiently summarized.
   - Interpretation, speculation, conclusions, or discussion are included where only reporting of results is appropriate.
   - Claims are supported by the reported data.
   - The section remains concise and focused on findings.

3. Identify only substantive issues that may affect:
   - Clarity
   - Scientific communication
   - Logical organization
   - AACR compliance
   - Reader understanding

4. For each issue identified, provide:
   - Exact Sentence(s): Quote the exact sentence(s) from my Results section that triggered the comment.
   - Problem: Explain the issue.
   - Why it matters: Explain why it may weaken the Results section or conflict with AACR expectations.
   - Suggested improvement: Describe what should be shortened, clarified, reorganized, added, or removed. Do not rewrite the entire section.

5. Do NOT rewrite the manuscript.

6. Do NOT perform grammar correction, copyediting, or language polishing unless the issue affects scientific clarity.

7. Only report issues that are genuinely important. Do not generate minor or speculative comments. If a section adequately satisfies AACR expectations, state that no revision is needed.

8. Pay particular attention to distinguishing:
   - Appropriate summary of data versus excessive restatement of figures and tables.
   - Presentation of findings versus interpretation of findings.
   - Reporting observations versus drawing conclusions better suited for the Discussion section.

Present your feedback using the following format:

Strengths:
- ...
- ...

Issues and Suggestions:

Issue 1
- Exact Sentence(s):
  "[quoted sentence(s)]"
- Problem:
- Why it matters:
- Suggested improvement:

Issue 2
- Exact Sentence(s):
  "[quoted sentence(s)]"
- Problem:
- Why it matters:
- Suggested improvement:

Results:
[PASTE TEXT HERE]
```
